## Scope
- Update only the homepage opening section; leave navigation, lower homepage sections, products, pricing, checkout, and footer untouched.
- Preserve the current headline, supporting text, research-use notice, buttons, and links.

## Design
- Create a restrained autumn setting with warm copper and burgundy light, sparse drifting leaves, and subtle depth.
- Reuse the existing `Vial` rendering for **GHK-Cu 100 MG**, preserving its exact BH label, vial proportions, cap, and blue powder.
- Reuse the existing BH logo asset as a large translucent watermark behind the foreground content without altering it.
- Keep the existing BH typography and professional research-focused visual language.

## Responsive behavior
- Desktop: balanced two-column composition with content and vial sharing the opening viewport.
- Tablet: compact two-column arrangement with controlled type and product sizing.
- Mobile: intentional stacked composition with readable headline, comfortable buttons, fully visible vial, and protected foreground zones.

## Motion and performance
- Use lightweight CSS-only motion for sparse falling leaves, ambient light, logo drift, and gentle vial float.
- Keep the label sharp and place effects away from important text and controls.
- Disable decorative movement when reduced motion is requested.

## Validation
- Check common desktop, tablet, and mobile dimensions for cropping, overlap, horizontal scrolling, excessive whitespace, and readable controls.
