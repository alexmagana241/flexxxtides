# Vial Label Strength Visibility

## Scope
- Change only the shared vial-label spacing.
- Preserve every vial design, product name, full strength, research wording, color, image, and all website content and behavior.

## Implementation
- Keep the product strength on its own unobstructed line.
- Position “FOR RESEARCH PURPOSES ONLY” slightly lower with fixed separation so neither line overlaps or clips at any rendered vial size.

## Verification
- Check every product and strength in the catalog and product views at desktop and phone widths.
- Confirm both the complete strength and exact research wording are visible on every vial and the site builds cleanly.
